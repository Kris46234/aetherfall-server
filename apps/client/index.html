RELEASE HISTORYAetherfall Change Log

Balance, combat rules, bot behaviour and interface improvements, recorded by release.

Version 2.32Infernal Performance
  • [Soulweaver] Chaos Bolt now has a 12 sec cooldown. Learning it also transforms Creeping Torment into Immolate, a 1 sec cast with immediate Fire damage and an 8 sec burn.
  • [Soulweaver] Press Essence Siphon again to cancel its channel. Its Chaos Bolt interaction appears only after Chaos Bolt is learned, and Fear now has a 6 sec cooldown.
  • [Stormwarden] Frost Shock keeps its damage mark for 8 sec, while its 25% snare lasts up to 3 sec and shares root/snare diminishing returns.
  • [Performance] Infernal landing and movement no longer create dynamic point lights or sort targets every frame. Particle counts, shadow work and long-lived effect overhead were reduced without removing the Infernal model.
  • [Interface] Gear-adjusted damage is written directly into the normal spell description instead of adding an oversized estimate panel.
  • [Visuals] Imported character models retain their calibrated foot offset so their feet meet the arena floor instead of hovering.
Version 2.31Chaos Reforged
  • [Soulweaver] Chaos Bolt replaces Unstable Affliction on the Soul Drain branch. It has a 1.45 sec cast, an 18 sec cooldown, always critically strikes and gains damage from Critical Strike chance.
  • [Soulweaver] Every Essence Siphon tick reduces Chaos Bolt cooldown by 2 sec, while its self-healing is increased by 30%. Dark Pact now shields for 20% maximum health.
  • [Shadowblade] Gouge lasts 3 sec and grants 10% overall damage when learned. Crimson Vial restores 1.5% maximum health each second for 10 sec, and Evasion grants 70% melee dodge.
  • [Shadowblade] Vendetta lasts 10 sec on a 60 sec cooldown. Shiv slows for 4 sec while its poison vulnerability lasts 8 sec; Viper Cut poison also lasts 8 sec.
  • [Interface] Direct-damage tooltips now show an estimate calculated from the equipped character’s gear, talents and active modifiers, with random critical strikes identified separately.
  • [Visuals] Chaos Bolt is a large green-and-black fel projectile. Crimson Vial now shows a drinking motion followed by persistent red restorative particles.
Version 2.30Fel Pursuit
  • [Infernal] Summon Infernal now uses a hulking grey-stone model with oversized armour, bright fel-green cores and cracks, and a matching fel impact. It actively chases the nearest enemy instead of standing at its landing point.
  • [Control] Gouge is now an incapacitate separate from Blind, while Blind uses the disorient track. The redundant purple frame banner is removed; the compact effect and remaining duration stay visible.
  • [Soulweaver] Mortal Horror and Shadowfury cost no mana; Soul Barrier, Fear and Soul Scar each cost 2 mana.
  • [Stormwarden] Healing Stream Totem lasts 10 sec and heals within 18m. Frost Shock slows by 25% for 8 sec and increases Arc Spark and Forked Current damage against that target by 15% for its duration.
  • [Windwalker] Rising Sun Kick performs a smooth backflip, while Cloudstep Dash uses a forward serpent-kick pose and spectral serpent trail.
  • [Interface] Summon Infernal now has dedicated action-bar and talent artwork.
  • [Fix] Healing Surge commits its cooldown only when the cast completes successfully; cancelling the cast by moving no longer wastes the ability.
  • [New] Soulweaver can learn Summon Infernal to place a killable 10 sec summon: its landing stuns for 2 sec, it pulses nearby enemies every 2 sec and restores 4 mana every second.
  • [Shadowblade] Crimson Vial restores 1% maximum health each second for 10 sec regardless of dampening. Gouge incapacitates for 2 sec, while Garrote's descriptions now clearly state that it does not silence.
  • [Balance] Healer spell costs use 95% of their original values. Lifesage healing is reduced by 10%; Soulweaver maximum mana is increased by 15% and all Soulweaver damage is reduced by another 10%.
  • [Stormwarden] Healing Stream Totem now has 280 health.
  • [Talents] Specialisation choices now describe their exact working effects. Windwalker's obsolete Cyclone wording is replaced by Flowing Steps and Finishing Technique.
  • [Interface] Action-bar arrangements are saved separately for each learned ability set, so switching builds and returning restores the correct buttons and key slots.
Version 2.28Spell Clarity & Ground Targeting
  • [Interface] Every baseline and talent spell now uses player-facing descriptions with clear targeting, timing and spell interactions instead of unexplained patch-note terms.
  • [New] Meteor and Shadowfury use movable ground-placement circles confirmed with left click; right click or Escape cancels. Shadowfury now stuns enemies in its area for 3 sec.
  • [Fix] Confirmed ground locations now pass safely into combat resolution, so Meteor falls and Shadowfury erupts instead of consuming their cost without producing an effect.
  • [Controls] Instant Camera Response removes intentional camera smoothing so mouse turns are applied to the rendered view on the very next frame. It is enabled by default and can be switched off in Settings.
  • [Lifesage] Renewal Tide empowers the next Verdant Mend by 150%. Lifesage and Paladin basic attacks are single deliberate left-click attacks instead of repeating on/off toggles.
  • [Shadowblade] Umbral Pounce grants 50% melee dodge for 1.5 sec, the damage check respects the displayed chance, and both Pounce and Evasion use brighter moving evasion trails.
  • [Talents] Previously inactive passive nodes now connect to combat calculations, and vague talent descriptions have been replaced with exact values.
  • [Balance] Healer spell costs used 85% of their original values, a ten-point increase from the previous 75% setting.
Version 2.27Recorded Expert AI
  • [AI] Discipline is calibrated from nine expert wins: maintain Atonement, use offensive Penance and Solace, weave Smite, reserve Shadow Mend for recovery, and answer split pressure with Radiance.
  • [AI] Paladin is calibrated from thirteen recorded matches: predictive Bestow Faith and Sacrifice, self-risk checks with Divine Protection, mana-positive Judgement, dynamic Divine Toll targets and safer Holy Light casts.
  • [AI] Shadowblade is calibrated from ten recorded matches: valid-cast interrupts, deliberate Blind setup, Garrote before Vendetta, defensive-aware burst and coordinated kill-target selection.
  • [Fix] Repaired missing Discipline Smite and Paladin enemy-healer references that could collapse those bot decision paths into inconsistent fallback play.
  • [Difficulty] Hard and high-rated bots execute the full recorded policies; lower difficulties retain slower reactions and less reliable setup conversion.
Version 2.25Atonement Discipline
  • [AI] Discipline now establishes and refreshes Atonement, then prioritises offensive Penance, Solace and Smite to heal through damage.
  • [AI] Shadow Mend is reserved for critical health, dangerous incoming burst, Holy-school lockouts, or a missing offensive line instead of being spammed whenever an ally is merely injured.
  • [AI] Healers briefly commit to one triage target unless another ally becomes substantially more urgent, reducing indecisive target swapping under split pressure.
  • [Balance] Shadow Mend healing is increased by 20%, from 238 to 286 before talents, gear and dampening.
  • [Online] Protocol 20 keeps the stronger Shadow Mend value synchronized between the browser and authoritative server.
Version 2.24Coordinated Regrouping
  • [AI] Healers and low-health teammates now publish one shared pillar regroup point, preventing the healer and DPS from retreating to opposite sides of the arena.
  • [AI] Split-pressure triage scores health loss, incoming burst, attackers and crowd control. Lifesage spreads both HoTs, Discipline spreads Atonement shields, and Paladin spreads Bestow Faith before damage becomes critical.
  • [AI] Non-Soulweaver DPS can spend appropriate control to peel a pressured healer and help the team cross to safety. The working Soulweaver rotation is unchanged.
  • [Balance] Fae Retreat now travels 9m. Power Shield, Penance, Psychic Scream and Shadow Mend cost 6, 8, 4 and 6 mana.
  • [Audio] Static Snare uses a distinct supplied dark-binding cue instead of sharing Static Field's ice sound.
Version 2.23Rated AI & Combat Clarity
  • [AI] Bot reactions now scale through five rating tiers. Expanded talents are evaluated after target, range and line-of-sight planning, preserving coordinated targets while reducing low-rated random cooldown use.
  • [AI] Stormkeeper, Skybreaker Pulse and Volcanic Eruption now form one ordered Stormwarden burst plan, with Healing Surge reserved for genuine recovery windows.
  • [Rule] Cauterize is a passive cheat-death again and grants its intended 50% movement burst. Warbreaker correctly adds 30% to the next Mortal Swing.
  • [Balance] Blinding Light lasts 5 seconds on a 45-second cooldown. Ordinary melee attacks gain a small reach allowance; ranged Stormbolt is unchanged.
  • [Visual] Alter Time displays a large clock whose twelve marks count down to the return. Shield Wall surrounds the Warrior with four rotating shield panels.
  • [Audio] Restored distinct supplied sounds for Volcanic Eruption, Arc Spark, Forked Current, Healing Surge, Gale Reversal, Static Snare, Wind Shear, Skybreaker Pulse and Tigereye Brew.
  • [Interface] Cooldowns use clear “Cooldown: 16s” labels. A new setting can hide live damage/healing meters during combat without removing the post-match report.
Version 2.22Smooth 3v3 & Storm Rotation
  • [Fix] Removed the v221 whole-HUD 30 Hz throttle and in-combat render-resolution switching that made 3v3 animation, cast bars and movement look choppy. Only the non-visual Details tables are rate-limited; the proven particle budget and batched cleanup remain.
  • [New] Volcanic Eruption now automatically launches two Lava Bursts after its main impact, each dealing roughly 60 damage.
  • [AI] Stormwarden now maintains Flame Shock only on its coordinated target, builds Skybreaker and Volcanic Eruption pressure, uses Forked Current and Arc Spark as fillers, and reserves Healing Surge for genuine emergencies.
  • [AI] Re-audited every class rotation against the current toolkit. Lifesage bots now recognise Blooming Echo and Rejuvenate as independent HoTs and can maintain both.
  • [Interface] Corrected the Spirit Blossom icon in the previous release notes.
Version 2.21Altered Time & Arena Performance
  • [New] Alter Time replaces Phoenix Guard. It records the Flame Duelist's health and position, then restores both after five seconds or on recast. Cooldown: 60 seconds.
  • [Balance] Warbreaker now empowers the next Mortal Swing by 30%. Touch of Death detonates for 30% of damage recorded during its five-second mark.
  • [Balance] Volcanic Eruption and Spirit Blossom deal or heal 20% and 10% more respectively. Volcanic Eruption now uses a dedicated eruption impact from the supplied audio pack.
  • [Balance] Totem Mastery grants 5% damage, healing, shielding and proc chance, plus 10% Flame Shock damage, at zero mana cost.
  • [Rule] Mortal Horror always heals its caster for 20% maximum health before dampening modifies the result.
  • [Audio] Windwalker control, utility and movement abilities now use distinct supplied samples: Incapacitate, Willow Guard, Valley Sweep, Tiger's Lust, Disabling Reach and Whirling Dragon Punch.
  • [Performance] 3v3 now uses adaptive render scale, fewer disposable particle objects and throttled HUD/detail updates during heavy combat.
  • [Interface] Training Grounds can only remain selected in Training queue, ability dragging accepts the nearest slot, Armoury class selectors show the new class art, and release notes now include ability icons.

Conquest Master Stat Guide

Quick reference for how combat stats influence your champion.

StaminaEvery 1 point adds 0.72 maximum health, combined with Vitality and capped at +420 HP.
Primary AttributeIntellect, Agility or Strength adds 0.01% damage and healing per point. Warrior now correctly uses Strength.
PowerEvery 1 point adds 0.038% damage. It does not secretly add another build stat.
RestorationEvery 1 point adds 0.038% healing.
VitalityEvery 1 point adds 0.72 maximum health, sharing the +420 HP cap with Stamina.
ManaEvery 1 point adds 0.18 maximum mana, capped at +60 mana.
VersatilityEvery 1 point adds 0.018% damage, 0.018% healing and 0.018% damage reduction. Its damage and healing bonuses continue above the 30% core-stat cap.
Critical StrikeChampions have a base 5% critical chance. Every 1 point adds 0.03%, up to 35% total. Critical damage and healing are 150% of normal.
Progression CurveStats now scale more sharply by item level: entry gear is deliberately modest, ilvl 950 stays near the previous combat midpoint, and elite 970–1000 gear gains increasingly stronger totals. Power/Restoration plus primary stats cap at 30%; Versatility continues beyond it.
Every forged item has automatic Core Attributes (Stamina + class primary) and exactly the two Build Bonuses you selected. No hidden recommended Power or Restoration is added as a third build bonus.
